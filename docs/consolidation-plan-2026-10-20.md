# Consolidation: one app, organised around making

*2026-10-20. A pass over the whole app after the Studio, Software for You and the free-software work. Checked against the [North Star](north-star.md) and the [Studio plan](studio-plan.md).*

## Phase 2, done (2026-10-20)

- **Names:** App Nav → **Goals**, Academy Classes → **Classes**, Portfolio Archives → **Showcase**, Practice games → **Practice**, "Commander" → **You** (until you set a name). Ids and routes are unchanged, so saved progress and links keep working. *App Nav was the founder's own name (2026-10-10); switching back is one label in featureCatalog.js.*
- **Missions:** one list (Library → Make → Missions; Learn; Home's card) with **Software for You** first and the ten-minute **quick missions** (were Quests) after. Quick missions open in Classes' screen and come back to Missions.
- **Grow it into a project:** on every Build. Makes a Workshop project with the kind's first real step as its next action, a "Grown from a Studio Build" milestone, and the link both ways (the Build then says "Open your project").
- **One Today card:** the Focus slot draws focus, Right now, what's on, the next thing on the desk and one check-in. Who gets which part (stages, picks, saved layouts) is untouched; Edit layout still shows them apart.
- **One card rule:** tinted = yours (Dream), edge = in progress (goal, a Build halfway, Right now), plain = everything else. Persona cards, You and Wisdom lost their decorative edges.
- **Home's order** for new layouts: Dream → Studio → Today's Find → **your goal** → **Today** → You. Saved layouts keep theirs.

## The short version

The app grew in layers. Each layer added its own way to **tell you what to do next**, its own place to **make things**, and its own **name**. Now the spine is clear (**Discover → Learn → Make → Do**, through the Studio), so the older layers can fold into it.

| Ways the app tells you **what to do next** | **10** | **1** next step |

## 1. "What do I do next?" — 10 systems → 1 next step

| System | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Goals | Home "Your goal" + "Your steps", the Compass ("App Nav") | One goal at a time, steps tick themselves |
| Stages | Home "What's next", stage unlocks | Opens more of the app as you go |
| First-goal guide | Overlay | Walks the first goal |
| Getting Started | Home card | Deferred onboarding |
| Right now | Home card | One line for this time of day |
| Challenges | Learn → Challenges | Daily/weekly XP missions |
| Daily Drills | Learn, Home card | Spaced practice |
| Quests | Classes, Home card | Spark → research → check → do |
| Software for You | Studio, Learn | Learn → see → practise → do |
| Studio "next part" | Home Studio card | Take the next part apart |

**The overlap that matters most:** **Quests and Software for You missions are the same shape** (teach → see/research → check → do a real thing). Two screens, two progress stores, two names.

**Recommend:**
1. **One word: Missions.** Quests and Software for You become one list in Learn, one screen (the Mission screen shows things live; Quests gain that).
2. **Challenges fold into Goals and Drills.** Daily XP missions ("play 3 rounds") compete with goals for attention and reward activity, not progress. Keep the drills; retire the XP missions.
3. **One next step on Home.** Priority: a Build or mission you're halfway through → your goal's next step → a drill that's due. Everything else is one tap deeper.
4. **Rename "App Nav" to "Goals".** It's where goals live; nobody looks for "App Nav".

## 2. Making — 3 places → 1 Make page ✅ done

Studio (the app makes it with you), The Workshop (your own projects), Idea Garden (before a project) and Portfolio (finished) were split across Library → Find and Library → Tools, plus three Home cards (Active Projects, Latest Ideas, Founder Quest).

**Done now:** a **Make** page in the Library: **Studio · Software for You · The Workshop · Portfolio · Idea Garden**. Guide paths, tours and Help point to it. Find is now purely discovery; Tools is purely planning and keeping.

**Next:**
- **"Grow it into a project"** on a Build → a Workshop project with its Build attached. The bridge from *the app made it* to *you're making it*.
- **Retire the Idea Garden** into the Workshop's Idea stage (projects already have one; it's already an extra).
- **Portfolio → "Showcase"**: Builds are already on it; finished projects too. One shelf.

## 3. Finding yourself — 5 places → 1 "You" page

Dream (Home), Wayfinder (values, Big Five), Sparks (Studio: what you keep making), Career Explorer, Life Area ratings.

**Recommend:** one **You** page: **your Dream** on top → **what you're drawn to** (Sparks) → **what matters to you** (Wayfinder values) → **where it could lead** (careers). Each still opens its full screen. This is "find yourself" in one scroll, in your own words and your own receipts.

## 4. Saving — 4 places → 1 "Saved"

Capture Inbox, Knowledge Vault (notes, research, tools), Finds (a tab inside the Vault), Bring stuff in / Fill with AI.

**Recommend:** the Vault becomes **Saved** with three tabs: **Inbox** (Capture) · **Saved** · **Finds**. Bring stuff in stays a button inside it.

## 5. Today — 7 Home cards → 1

Commander, Focus & Calendar, Today's Activities, On the Desk, Right now, Check-ins, plus persona cards (Habits, Study Blocks, Systems Check, Recurring Ops...).

**Recommend:** **one Today card**: today's focus, the next 3 things on the Planner, and one check-in if it's due. Home becomes: **Dream → your one next step → Studio → Today's Find → Today**. Persona cards stay pickable in My features, off by default.

## 6. Progress — 5 places → 1 "Receipts"

Insights (Stats), Portfolio stats, Profile level, Sparks, Life Area then-vs-now (planned).

**Recommend:** one **Receipts** page: what you made, what you changed, what you mastered, how your life areas moved. Their own numbers (North Star: "their own receipts"). Levels and points sit under it, never above.

## 7. Practice — make it personal

Practice games, Daily Drills, Challenges, Studio part checks, mission practice, Tech Lab.

**Recommend:** drills pull from **what you made and learned** (the parts you mastered, the missions you did), not random trivia. Tech Lab now carries the Software for You ideas; the rest follows.

## 8. Your character, pet and room

The room appears in Learn (hero stage), the Studio (stage) and Profile (wardrobe, Decorate, pet). That's fine as **views of one room**. Keep editing in **one** place: Profile → "Your room". Trophies from Builds already land there.

## 10. The tab bar

Today: **Library · Home · Learn**. Making, the spine, is two levels deep.

**Recommend:** **Home · Make · Learn · Library**. Make becomes a tab (the Studio, your projects, Showcase). Library keeps Life, Find and Tools. This is the biggest change here and the one that most says what the app is.

## 11. How it looks, across every way people customise it

Checked on 2026-10-20 in **Plain + light + teal** and **Command + dark + gold**, and with Home on **Just my picks**, **All in** and **Calm**.

**Fixed now:**
- The **Studio card** on Home ran edge to edge while every other card has side margins.
- The **beat's step grid** overflowed at phone width, and its title was clipped in a part's small preview.
- **Previews couldn't scroll on phones**, so longer Builds (the business offer) were cut off. Only the game keeps scrolling off (it takes drags).
- **MAKE SOMETHING** and **CLASSES** on Learn were white text on bright mint and lavender in dark mode. They now pick white or dark text by contrast.

**What holds up:** Command style turns the new labels into mono capitals and Plain keeps them calm, with no hard-coded fonts or colours (the style check covers every new screen). Calm Home puts the Studio inside its own panel in the Calm voice. Just my picks hides what you didn't pick, and the Make page offers "Add them in My features".

**Recommend:**

| Today | Better |
|---|---|
| **Home's first screen is 7 cards** before your goal: Dream, Studio, Today's Find, Right now, Commander, focus, goal | **Dream → one next step → Today's Find**, then one Today card (§5). The first screen should say one thing |
| **Four card treatments on one screen**: tinted (Dream), plain (Studio, Find), left edge (Right now, goal), gold top edge (Commander) | **One rule:** tinted = **yours** (Dream). Edge = **in progress** (your goal, a Build halfway). Plain = everything else |
| **Learn is titled "Commander"** and shows rank chips first; Home's card is "Commander" too | Your **name**, and what you're making first. Points and tier move to Receipts (§6) |
| **Hub colours are fixed** (Find teal, Make purple, Tools gold) while the accent is the person's choice | Keep hub colours as **small** identity marks only (an icon, an edge), and let buttons always follow the accent, as they do now |
| **Studio can't be switched off** in My features | Keep it that way, like the Dream and Today's Find: it's the spine, not a tool. Say so in My features ("Always on: Dream, Studio, Today's Find") |
| **Three stages show your character** (Learn, Studio, Profile) at different sizes | One size and one placement for the room: a short banner at the top of Learn and the Studio, editing only in Profile (§8) |
