# Deskartes North Star

*Written 2026-10-17 from the founder's own words. Every screen, feature and PR is checked against this.*

**Mission:** help people **discover who they really are**, **find a dream they can reach**, and **walk the way there**: on **their** terms, not the system's.

**The promise:** *Nobody has all the answers. We walk with you while you find yours.*

---

## What we believe

| Belief | What it means in the app |
|---|---|
| **The why comes first** | Every task ties back to the person's **own dream**. They should always see **why**. |
| **Their dream, not the system's** | The app **reflects** their dream. It never writes it for them. XP, levels and suggested goals sit **below** the dream, never above it. |
| **Passion is built, not found** | **Interest follows doing.** Never ask *"what's your passion?"* Hand them **small experiments** instead. |
| **Hope has 3 parts** | **Something to want** + **a way there** + **belief you can**. We're strong on the way. We build the other two. |
| **Bursts, not routines** | People are spontaneous. The app **holds the thread** between bursts. **No guilt** for gaps. |
| **Lead and teach** | Show **how the world works** (money, work, attention, health) so people can navigate it and **still be themselves**. |
| **A feeling, not a feature** | People forget features. They remember **how the app made them feel about themselves**. |
| **Trust is earned** | Faith comes from **kept promises**, **honesty** and making people **stronger**, not hooked. |
| **Software's power, for everyone** | People who aren't engineers finally get to **see what software can do for them**, without paying **license fees** to find out. Show them by **making it with them**, and point them to the **free** tools that go further. |

## Software's power, for everyone

*In the founder's words (2026-10-20):*

> I just like that non-engineer people can finally witness what awesome things software can do for them if they don't have to pay extensive money and license costs for it.

**What it means in the app:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **See it work first** | The Studio **makes the thing with you** (a page, a game, a beat, a business, an AI helper). Nobody has to believe software can help: they watch it. |
| **No license wall** | What they make is **one file, theirs to keep**: no account, no subscription, no license. Said plainly on every Build. |
| **AI, demystified** | The **AI helper** Build teaches what makes AI useful (a role, a tone, an answer shape) and **honest** (it can sound sure and be wrong). It runs on **free** AI chats. |
| **Free tools to go further** | Every Build ends with **Go further, free**: open-source and free software that does what people usually pay for (Godot, LMMS, Krita, LibreOffice...). |
| **Free instead of paid** | Finds include free software, each card saying what it stands in for: *"Free instead of a paid photo editor."* |
| **Honest about "free"** | Name the catch: a free *tier*, a big learning curve, a computer it needs. Never promise a dollar figure we haven't checked. |

## The shape of the way

> **Dream:** the life you want, **in your words**
> ↓
> **Path:** the projects and goals that build it
> ↓
> **Today:** one step

**The why runs through all three.** A step shows the dream it builds. The dream shows the steps that built it.

## The voice: a mentor, not a game

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| *"Quest Complete!"* | *"You finished it. **Most people never start.**"* |
| *"You reached Level 4"* | *"Three things built this month. **You're becoming a builder.**"* |
| *"Your streak is at risk"* | Nothing. Or, when they come back: *"**Welcome back.** The next step is ready."* |
| *"What's your passion?"* | *"Describe a normal **Tuesday** in the life you want."* |
| *"3 overdue"* | *"Last time you were halfway through **X**."* |

- **Name who they're becoming.** Identity over points: *builder*, *someone who finishes*.
- **Respect, never scold.** Users are up against **billion-dollar attention engineering**. They aren't dumb, and they are never "cattle".
- **Short and bold.** No text blocks (see the copy rule).

## The screen test

Ask of **every** screen, feature and PR:

1. Does it make them feel **more capable**, or **more managed**?
2. Does it **pull strings**, or **cut them**?
3. Can they see **why** they're doing it?
4. Does it work for someone who shows up **in bursts**, after **any** gap?
5. Is this **their** want, or **ours**?
6. Does it make them **stronger**, or just **more hooked**?

## Where it lives in the app

| Area | Today | North Star |
|---|---|---|
| **The dream** | **Doesn't exist.** The app never asks for the life someone wants in their words. | A **top layer** above everything: their dream, their values, what they **won't trade**. Rough is fine. It can change. |
| **Onboarding** | *"What did you come here for?"* picks a tool path | Asks for the dream with **concrete** questions. Wayfinder answers **draft it**: *"Sound like you?"* |
| **Wayfinder values** | Picked once, then **buried** in the Wayfinder | Shown on Home. Used as a **filter** for big choices. |
| **Home** | Dashboard of cards, streak up front | **Dream at the top.** *Right now* shows the **why**. **Welcome back**, not catch-up. |
| **Projects + goals** | Tasks and steps | Each one **ladders up**: *"This builds toward **the farm**."* |
| **Celebrations** | *Quest Complete*, levels, points | **Mentor lines.** Who they're **becoming**. |
| **Reminders** | Daily drills, *"streak at risk"* | **Gifts only:** a resurfaced idea, **their own words**, a tip for their project. |
| **Streaks** | Everywhere. Tutorial: *"The streak is the one that matters."* | **Momentum** (what you did), never missed-day counts. Optional at most. |
| **Classes** | Lessons | **Why before how.** Map the system. **Builder stories**: neighbors, not celebrities. |
| **Proof** | Life-area ratings **overwrite** the old score, so there's no "before" | **Then vs now**: their own receipts. Needs a rating history. |
| **The founder** | Absent | **Your story at the front door.** Short, honest: *"I fall for it too."* |
| **Clarity moments** | Nothing | One tap to write what they see. It comes back **when the fog returns**. |

## Keeping the promise

- **Promise small and specific.** Something they can check **in minutes**. A changed life is a **pile of kept promises**.
- **Value before signup.** Let a guest get something real first.
- **Their own receipts.** Show what **they** did, in **their** numbers.
- **Name what it takes.** Never *"life-changing"*.
- **Find people at a turning point.** They already want to walk. They need **the path**.

---

## Grounding

- **Passion is developed:** O'Keefe, Dweck & Walton (2018). People who think passion is *found* drop interests when they get hard.
- **Hope theory:** C.R. Snyder: goals + pathways + agency.
- **Self-belief:** Albert Bandura. It grows most from **your own wins**, then from **seeing people like you** win.
