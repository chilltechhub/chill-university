## 1. The big picture in one breath

**Wide vision, narrow first version.**

- **The vision:** help anyone live better with useful knowledge: fix their life, learn, and use what they learn. Eventually for every stage of life, and eventually as **a school**: you, staff, students and groups, online and in real life.
- **The first version:** **young adults, starting with 17–18-year-olds leaving high school** and entering the adult world. A few paths, made deep and great.
- **How it grows:** one engine, new **content packs** (a new stage, a new path) added on top. Not new features.

## 3. Core theme: ikigai

**Ikigai** (生き甲斐): roughly **"a reason for being"**, what makes life worth living.

Use **both** versions, and be honest about which is which (the North Star's **Trust** belief):

| Version | What it is | What it does for us |
|---|---|---|
| **The Japanese meaning** | Small, everyday reasons: your morning, your people, your craft. You don't need one big passion (Ken Mogi, *Awakening Your Ikigai*) | Takes the pressure off. Fits **bursts, not routines** and **passion is built, not found** |
| **The four-circle diagram** | **Love · Good at · World needs · Paid for**, with ikigai where they overlap. A Western mash-up from 2014 (a Spanish purpose diagram + the Japanese word) | A **map** of your life that's easy to explain in 30 seconds |

**Supporting ideas** (to explain it, not to add features):

| Idea | One line | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Wheel of Life** | Rate each part of life 1–10 and see what's low | Already in the app as life-area ratings |
| **Maslow** | You can't chase a dream while the basics are on fire | Why the ground comes first for some people |
| **Self-Determination Theory** | People thrive with **choice**, **getting good**, **connection** | The why behind *"more capable, not more managed"* |

### The 8 life areas feed the circles

| Area | Plain words | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical** | Body: sleep, food, movement, energy | **The ground** |
| **Mental** | Head: stress, mood, focus | **The ground** |
| **Digital** | Tech: phone, privacy, your tools | **The ground** |
| **Creative** | What you make | Love · Good at |
| **Social** | Your people | Love · World needs |
| **Spiritual** | Meaning, faith, values | Love · World needs |
| **Professional** | Work, skills, career, business | Good at · Paid for |
| **Financial** | Money in, out, saved | Paid for |

> **Life areas are how you get there. Ikigai is where you're headed.** Every Find, lesson and step lands on an area; every area feeds a circle.

**The feature test:** *which circle (or the ground) does this help?* None → it's parked.

💡 **Naming:** the North Star's open question (*Your Why / The Dream / The Way*) — the Dream layer **is** their ikigai. Plain-English name on screen, ikigai as the explained idea behind it.

## 4. The engine: one loop for every path

People can't pick a dream yet: **you can't know you like something you've never tried.** So every path starts with a map and tastes.

| Step | They ask | The app gives |
|---|---|---|
| **1. See the map** | "What's even out there?" | Every option in this world, plainly, sorted into a few types (cost, speed, risk, what your day looks like) |
| **2. Taste it** | "What do I even like?" | Small tries: **online** (an hour, free) and **real life** (an afternoon, local, with a script) |
| **3. Notice** | "Did that pull me in?" | **Want more · Not for me · Not sure** + one line in their words. A pattern reflected back after a few |
| **4. Pick and walk** | "Where do I start?" | The direct path, built on something they actually tried |
| **5. Grow** | "What's next?" | **The map gets bigger** as they do. Unlock *more of the map*, not more features |

**Template that already exists:** Career Explorer (`src/screens/library/careerexplore.js`): what the work is, how people get there, tools, a staged roadmap, where to go. Build the other maps the same way.

**The core every path uses:** Dream (why) · Finds (the tool) · a short lesson (understand) · one next step (do) · the Vault (keep). **Everything else is a path's kit.**

### Example map: types of business

| Type | Examples | To start |
|---|---|---|
| **Service** | Cleaning, tutoring, lawn care, hair, repairs | A skill + a phone |
| **Make a product** | Candles, baking, clothing, furniture | Materials + somewhere to sell |
| **Resell** | Thrift flipping, wholesale, sneakers | Some cash + an eye for deals |
| **Digital product** | Templates, courses, beats, apps | A skill + a computer |
| **Content** | YouTube, TikTok, podcast, newsletter | Time + patience |
| **Local shop** | Food truck, café, barbershop | More money + more risk |
| **Freelance** | Design, editing, bookkeeping, web | A sellable skill + a portfolio |
| **Buy one** | Laundromat, vending, a local service | Money or financing |

Tastes: *online* (a day-in-the-life video, one tiny version, run the numbers, read what owners complain about) · *real life* (ask a local owner 3 questions, walk a craft fair, do it once for someone you know, sell one thing on Marketplace).

## 5. First audience: 17–18, leaving high school

✅ **Young adults need a path.** The moment between high school and the adult world is the biggest turning point, and the North Star says *find people at a turning point.*

**Their real questions:**
- What do I do after high school? (college, trade, work, military, gap year, start something)
- How does money work when it's mine? (first paycheck, bank, credit, taxes)
- How do I get a job? (resume, interview, first job)
- How do I live on my own? (renting, cooking, insurance, healthcare)
- Who am I, and what do I actually like?

✅ **First paths (decided 2026-10-21):** **After high school** and **Money on my own**, with **What do I like?** as the default for anyone unsure. *First job* and *Living on my own* come next.

All candidate paths:

| Path | The map | Example tastes |
|---|---|---|
| **After high school** | College · community college · trade school · apprenticeship · work · military · gap year · start something | Shadow a trade for a day, a campus visit, a free intro course |
| **Money on my own** | Bank accounts · budgeting · credit · taxes · saving · first investing | Open a free account, read your first pay stub, check a credit score |
| **First job / career** | Career fields (Career Explorer) · resumes · interviews · side income | One informational chat, a mock interview, one application |
| **Living on my own** | Renting · groceries and cooking · insurance · healthcare · paperwork | Price out a real apartment, cook one week of meals on a budget |
| **What do I like?** | A sampler from all of the above | One taste a week, online or local |

### Starting points: same destination, different start ✅ (added 2026-10-21)

The education side covers **everyone in this age range**, not just kids on the usual track. They all head the same way (*making it on my own*), from different starts. **One new welcome question picks the start** (💡 wording: *"Where are you starting from?"*), and the paths above adapt to it.

✅ **v1 starting points (decided 2026-10-21):** **In high school · Didn't finish school (GED) · Want a trade.** They reuse the most of what's built (Academy classes = GED subjects, Career Explorer, Home Ec & Workshop). *Homeschooled, re-entry and never shown* come next.

| Starting point | What they need first | Built from (exists) | Missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **In high school** | After-high-school map, money basics | Academy classes, Career Explorer | Maps (§4) |
| **Homeschooled** | **State rules** for a homeschool diploma, a transcript, credits, getting into college or a trade with one | Academy classes | A homeschool guide by state |
| **Didn't finish school** | **GED or HiSET**, adult diploma programs, community college routes | **Math, Language Arts, Science, Social Sciences classes = the GED's 4 subjects** (Math, Reasoning Through Language Arts, Science, Social Studies) | GED prep framed as a path, practice tests, a state rules lookup |
| **Want a trade** | Trade types, **paid apprenticeships**, short certificates | Career Explorer (spans non-tech), Home Ec & Workshop class | A trades map (§4 style) |
| **Coming home from prison** (re-entry) | **ID documents first**, a job with a record, housing, record sealing, banking, the tech that changed | Money lessons, Finds, Career Explorer | A re-entry path |
| **Never been shown** (sheltered, foster care aging out, never traveled) | Everyday adult basics: transit, travel, appointments, phone calls, renting, paperwork | Life areas, Home Ec, Finds | A "how the world works" path, plus real-life tastes with scripts |

**What each one covers (first pass, check every fact before shipping):**

- **GED / HiSET:** state-run high school equivalency tests. The GED has 4 subjects. The usual minimum age is 18, younger with conditions in many states. Rules and costs **vary by state**. Steps: pick the test the state uses → free practice test → study the weak subject → book it.
- **Homeschool diploma:** in many states the parent issues it; requirements **vary by state**. Keep a transcript (courses, credits, grades). Colleges and the military have their own rules for homeschool diplomas.
- **Trades:** electrician, plumbing, HVAC, welding, carpentry, CNA/medical assistant, CDL truck driving, cosmetology, auto. **Apprenticeships pay while you learn.** Quick certificates: OSHA 10, ServSafe, forklift, CPR/First Aid.
- **Basic job skills (everyone):** showing up, a simple resume, an interview, talking to a boss, reading a pay stub, your rights at work.
- **Re-entry:** birth certificate → Social Security card → state ID, in that order. Fair-chance employers. The Federal Bonding Program and the Work Opportunity Tax Credit (reasons an employer can say yes). Record sealing or expungement (**varies by state**). Parole conditions. A bank account, a phone, how apps work now. Pell Grants are open again to people in prison (since 2023).
- **Never been shown:** riding transit, booking a trip, making an appointment, calling an office, ordering, renting, filling out forms, what to do in an emergency.

**Rules for this side:**

- **No shame, ever.** *"You're not behind. You're starting from here."* The voice is a mentor (North Star), never scolding.
- **Plain words, low reading level.** Some of these people were let down by school.
- **Location matters:** GED, homeschool and record rules are state by state, so these paths ask **which state** (stays on the phone).
- **Every fact gets a source and a reviewed date** (the Trust job); law and test rules change.
- **Works on a cheap phone with weak data.** Re-entry and low-income users may not have much more.
- **Real-life tastes come with a script:** what to bring, what to say, what to ask.
- 💡 **Partners for the school (§8):** adult education centers, libraries, re-entry programs, workforce centers and trade unions are natural meetup and staff partners.

**Constraint:** 17-year-olds are **minors**. v1 is 13+ (`docs/store-submission.md`), with age bands on Finds and parent consent at signup. Mentors, community and meetups keep their minor rules (Mentors already refuses minors).

## 6. Welcome before login ✅

**Today:** open app → Login. Create account → 8 onboarding cards → Home. **Guests skip onboarding entirely** and get the generic app, because onboarding writes straight to `profiles`, which needs an account (`src/screens/LoginScreen.js`, the guest button).

**New order:**

```
Open app → Welcome (ikigai, 3 screens) → "Which feels shakiest?"
        → First use: one Find or one step, matched to them
        → "Want to keep this?" → Create account (or keep going as a guest)
        → Home, already tailored
```

| Piece | What changes |
|---|---|
| **Answers saved on the phone first** | Copied into the account at signup. Part exists: the local Dream (`loadLocalDream`), cached guest points |
| **Login moves later** | Framed as "save your progress". A small **"I already have an account"** link on the welcome |
| **Returning users** | Signed in → skip the welcome |
| **Age** | One light range question early (content is age-banded). Full birth date and parent consent stay at signup. **Nothing personal leaves the phone before then** |
| **The 8 onboarding cards** | Most move after signup or go. The welcome does the tailoring |
| 💡 **Two questions become one** | Today: aim (9 options, several app-shaped like *"Keep my info in one place"*) **+** account type. New: the welcome answer; account type is worked out, not asked |

## 7. The first questions (in person and in the app)

### The spoken intro (~30 seconds)

> *"You know how some people just seem to have it figured out? There's a Japanese idea for that: **ikigai**. It means your reason for getting up in the morning."*
>
> *"It's usually four things overlapping: **what you love, what you're good at, what the world needs, and what you can get paid for.** Most of us have one or two down and the rest is fuzzy."*
>
> *"And under all that, your **body, your head and your money** have to be okay, or none of it works."*
>
> *"So out of those, **which one feels the shakiest for you right now?**"*

| They say | Ask next | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| **What I love** — "I don't know" | "What did you love doing as a kid?" | Tastes, Dream card, Today's Find |
| **What I'm good at** | "What do people ask you for help with?" | Classes, Career Explorer |
| **What the world needs** | "What problem makes you angry?" | Volunteering tastes, community |
| **Getting paid** | "Earning more, or keeping more?" | The money door (below) |
| **The ground** (body / head / money is a mess) | "Which one first?" | That life area, one small fix |

### The three doors (the quick version)

**"Is it more time, money, or figuring out what's next?"**

| Door | Follow-up | Show |
|---|---|---|
| **Money** | Spending less · earning more · debt? | Money Finds · Business classes + Workshop · Debt & Credit |
| **Time** | What eats your time: work, phone, all over the place? | Save-time Finds · Screen Time & Focus + Work Mode · Home's next step |
| **What's next** | Something in mind, or figuring it out? | Career Explorer · Startup class · Academy classes · Dream card + Today's Find |

**Close in 60 seconds:** open it on their phone (guest) → show **one** feature → give **one** 10-minute step → "Tell me how it went next time."

**Rules:** ask, then pitch one thing · use their words back · show, don't tell · guest first.

**In the app:** the same flow is the welcome. (1) the four circles and the ground, with a "where'd this come from?" link · (2) "Which feels shakiest?" · (3) one thing to try.

## 8. The school 💡

The app as **an extension of your knowledge and others'**: a course, a class, your own school.

| A school has | In Deskartes |
|---|---|
| **Teacher** (you) | You write the maps, Finds and lessons. Finds and lessons are **live-editable from Supabase**, no app update |
| **Staff** | Mentors who know one world well |
| **Students** | Anyone on a path |
| **Groups** | Cohorts: "class of 2027 after-high-school", a family |
| **Curriculum** | The maps and paths |
| **Campus** | Meet and greets, workshops, real-life tastes |
| **Office hours** | One-on-one conversations |
| **Graduation** | A student who walked a path **becomes staff** for the next. The top of the growth ladder is teaching it |

### The conversation (meet someone, find a way to help)

1. **"Walk me through a normal day."**
2. **"What costs you the most: time, money, or stress?"**
3. **"What's something you wish you'd known sooner?"**
4. **"What have you already tried?"**
5. **"If one thing got better this month, what would it be?"**

→ one Find + one step, maybe a path. **The same questions are the app's onboarding:** what you ask in person, the app asks when you're not there.

### Already built, switched off (`place: 'off'` in `featureCatalog.js`)

| Built | Does |
|---|---|
| **Organizations & Cohorts** (`src/screens/organization/`) | Create a school or group, invite codes, roster with progress, assignments |
| **Lesson Plan Builder** (`src/screens/LessonBuilder.js`) | Build a class from a content bank |
| **Mentors** (`MentorsScreen.js`) | Structured mentor requests; refuses minors; no in-app payment |
| **Community, Fellow Scholars, Feed** | Peer connections |
| **Family** (`src/screens/family/`) | A parent sees a child's progress |

### How to start it

**Run the school in real life first; the app holds the thread between meetups.** One meet and greet, ~10 people → the conversation → each leaves with one Find + one step in the app → follow up in a week. Switch features back on **only when real people need them** (avoids empty rooms and unplanned risk).

## 9. Tailoring rules ✅

> **Your dream picks your kit.** The core is always there. Kits show only for the path you're on. Change paths and the kit changes.

- **One stage** (from the age question)
- **One shakiest circle** (from the welcome)
- **One path at a time** (the Wayfinder already enforces one objective at a time in SQL)
- **One next step** on Home
- Everything else is out of sight until asked for, or until the map grows

**Too much vs too weak:** tailoring stops it being too much; **depth** stops it being too weak. A perfectly tailored shallow path still feels thin, which is why we launch narrow (§1).

### Stages of life (content packs on one engine)

| Stage | Big question | "Education" means |
|---|---|---|
| **Teen** (13–17) | Who am I? What am I good at? | School, first money, trying lots |
| **Young adult** (18–30) ← **first** | How do I make it on my own? | Career, money basics, first business, living alone |
| **Building** (30–50) | How do I get ahead or change course? | Career moves, family, investing, starting something |
| **Later life** (50+) | What matters now? | Health, meaning, giving back, keeping up with tech |

## 12. Philosophy reminders

- **Life can't be fully optimized, and shouldn't be.** Optimizing a life ends in managing the person. The aim is **a little better, by their own measure**: one useful thing learned, one step, one problem solved.
- **Everyone benefits from more useful information**, and it never runs out.
- **The account is the reward, not the gate.**
- **Ask before you pitch. Pitch one thing.**
